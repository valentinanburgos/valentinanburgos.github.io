UNICEF USA Youth Advocacy Training

WASHINGTON, D.C.
WASHINGTON, D.C.

The Washington Center: Career Launch Bootcamp

As a Dr. Nancy Cade Scholar, I participated in The Washington Center’s Career Launch Bootcamp in Washington, D.C., where I heard from HR professionals at Georgetown University and Airbus. Their insights helped me recognize HR as a career aligned with my interests in psychology and people-centered leadership. Daily workshops strengthened my career development, networking, and understanding of my strengths through psychometric assessments. Learning alongside peers from across the country broadened my perspective and clarified my career goals.

Career planning New job perspectives
JAPAN
TOKYO, HIROSHIMA, OSAKA, & KYOTO, JAPAN

Study Abroad: Cultural Anthropology

Through my course project and paper, I explored the relationship between French and Japanese cultures and how cultural values shape everyday life. Traveling throughout Japan was unforgettable, especially visiting Hiroshima. At a traditional tea ceremony, I learned the motto 一期一会 (ichigo ichie), meaning “once in a lifetime.” I still hold this philosophy close to my heart as a reminder to cherish the unique experiences every day brings.

Japanese language exposure Sociocultural and historical education
USA & JORDAN
ONLINE, USA & JORDAN

IREX Global Solutions Conversations

Through a six-week virtual exchange with students from Jordan, I explored the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and how global challenges affect communities in both the United States and the Middle East. Through cross-cultural discussions and design thinking, we examined shared sustainability challenges, compared our communities’ perspectives, and brainstormed local project ideas aligned with the SDGs. The experience strengthened my global awareness, cross-cultural communication, and understanding of how international collaboration can drive meaningful change.
