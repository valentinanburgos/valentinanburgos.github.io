AUSTRIA
SALZBURG & VIENNA, AUSTRIA

Global Citizenship Alliance Seminar

Through Miami Dade Honors College’s annual attendance to the Global Citizenship Alliance Seminar (February–March 2024), I was selected to participate in cohort-based learning exploring global architecture, pop music’s influence on society, and the history of labor around the world. I also researched and presented on climate refugeeism, deepening my understanding of global challenges and strengthening my ability to approach complex issues through cross-cultural perspectives.

USA
ONLINE, USA

UNICEF USA Youth Advocacy Training

Through UNICEF USA’s Youth Advocacy Training (July–August 2024), I developed a stronger understanding of youth advocacy and how young people can influence public policy and advance children’s rights. The experience introduced me to strategies for civic engagement, communicating with decision-makers, and using my voice to support meaningful change. It strengthened my commitment to youth empowerment and showed me how informed, organized advocacy can connect local action with broader global issues.

THE NETHERLANDS & BELGIUM
THE NETHERLANDS & BELGIUM

Game Show: The Quiz With Balls

My experience on Quiz with Balls in the Netherlands and Belgium challenged me to step outside my comfort zone, adapt to new cultural environments, and communicate across language barriers. While abroad, I continued studying Japanese online, logging into classes around midnight local time, while building my French skills and learning some Dutch to connect with the people around me. Balancing an international television experience with language learning taught me to stay adaptable, embrace unfamiliar situations, and pursue my goals wherever I am.

WASHINGTON, D.C.
WASHINGTON, D.C.

The Washington Center: Career Launch Bootcamp

As a Dr. Nancy Cade Scholar, I participated in The Washington Center’s Career Launch Bootcamp in Washington, D.C., where I heard from HR professionals at Georgetown University and Airbus. Their insights helped me recognize HR as a career aligned with my interests in psychology and people-centered leadership. Daily workshops strengthened my career development, self-awareness, networking, and understanding of my strengths through psychometric assessments. Learning alongside peers from across the country broadened my perspective and clarified my career goals.

Cross-cultural learning New perspectives
JAPAN
TOKYO, HIROSHIMA, OSAKA, & KYOTO, JAPAN

Study Abroad: Cultural Anthropology

Through my course project and paper, I explored the relationship between French and Japanese cultures and how cultural values shape everyday life. Traveling throughout Japan was unforgettable, especially visiting Hiroshima. At a traditional tea ceremony, I learned the motto 一期一会 (ichigo ichie), meaning “once in a lifetime.” I still hold this philosophy close to my heart as a reminder to cherish the unique experiences every day brings.
