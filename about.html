A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

More than what's on paper.

I'm Tina, a cat-mom to my precious cat Missifu, a middle sister to two amazing women, a black belt in Tae Kwon Do, an avid traveller, and an actress!

As a middle sister, I've learned a thing or two about mitigating conflicts and working as a team. As a cat-mom, I've learned responsibility and patience (especially in teaching my cat how to sit and do tricks). As a traveller, I've learned to embrace the new and do my due diligence in researching the countries I'm visiting beforehand to learn basic words in their national language and basic respectful customs. As an actress for over 10 years, I've learned to think on my feet, execute change, and work cross-functionally between props, costumes, crew, directors, and cast.

02 / WHAT DRIVES ME

The things I care about.

The themes that consistently shape the work I choose and the way I hope to show up for others.

✳

Understanding people

Growing up with two incredible, unique sisters has taught me that everyone has their own personality, perspective, and way of seeing the world. Those everyday experiences have shaped my curiosity about human behavior and how people connect, communicate, and work together.

♡

Leading with empathy

As a cat mom and lifelong animal lover, I've learned that care takes patience, consistency, and a little creativity—especially when teaching my cat tricks. I believe everyone deserves understanding and grace, because life happens, and we all need a little compassion sometimes.

↗

Turning ideas into action

More than a decade in acting and martial arts has taught me the importance of lifelong learning, skill development, discipline, adaptability, and calculated risk-taking. Behind the scenes, collaborating with cast, crew, directors, and design teams showed me how different people and moving parts come together to bring an idea to life.

⌘

Learning continuously

Traveling has taught me to approach new cultures with curiosity and respect. Before visiting a country, I research its customs and learn basic phrases in the local language. These small steps help me appreciate different perspectives, navigate unfamiliar environments thoughtfully, and embrace my role as a global citizen.
