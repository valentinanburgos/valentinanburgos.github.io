02 / WHAT DRIVES ME

What I care about.

I'm Tina: a middle sister, cat mom, actress, Tae Kwon Do black belt, and avid traveler!

✳

Understanding people

Growing up with two incredible, unique sisters has taught me that everyone has their own personality and way of seeing the world. Those everyday experiences shape my curiosity about understanding others and how to best connect, communicate, and work together.

♡

Leading with empathy

As a cat mom, I've learned that building trust takes consistency and perseverance (especially when teaching my cat new tricks). I believe people grow best when they're met with patience, compassion, and encouragement, just like my cat Missifu.

↗

Creativity and collaboration

10+ years in acting and martial arts have taught me the importance of lifelong learning and discipline. Collaborating with cast, technical teams, and directors has taught me how cross-functional collaboration can bring ideas to life. (Photo: as Jane Doe in Ride the Cyclone)

⌘

Cultural curiosity

I approach every culture with curiosity and respect. Before visiting a new country, I research local customs and learn basic phrases in the language, helping me appreciate different perspectives, connect with others, and navigate unfamiliar environments thoughtfully.
