A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

More than what's on paper.

I'm Tina, a cat-mom to my precious cat Missifu, a middle sister to 2 amazing women, and an avid traveller and actress!

01 / MY STORY

Curiosity has always been my starting point.

I'm currently studying Psychology at the University of Florida, focusing on Behavioral and Cognitive Neuroscience, with a minor in Linguistics.

I'm fascinated by how people think, communicate, learn, and navigate the environments around them. Those interests have led me toward research, student leadership, accessibility initiatives, and the study of people in organizations.

I enjoy work that combines analytical thinking with human connection — understanding a problem, listening to different perspectives, and finding practical ways to make something better.

The things I care about.

The themes that consistently shape the work I choose and the way I hope to show up for others.

Understanding people

Growing up with two amazing sisters has taught me that everyone has their own personality, perspective, and way of seeing the world. Those everyday experiences have shaped my curiosity about human behavior and how people connect, communicate, and work together.

♡

Being caring and patient to everyone

As a cat mom and lifelong animal lover, I've learned that care takes patience, consistency, and a little creativity—especially when teaching my cat tricks. I believe everyone deserves understanding and grace, because life happens, and we all need a little compassion sometimes.

Turning ideas into action

More than a decade in acting and martial arts has taught me life-long learning and skill-development, discipline, adaptability, and calculated risk-taking. Behind the scenes, collaborating with cast, crew, directors, and design teams showed me how different people and moving parts come together to bring an idea to life.

Learning continuously

Traveling has taught me to approach new cultures with curiosity and respect. Before visiting a country, I research its customs and learn basic phrases in the local language.
